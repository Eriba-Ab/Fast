# Surface brief: Taraba Central Billing and Collections (taraba-billing.html)

Mode: Operate. Audience: TIRS revenue managers and officers on office PCs, daytime, often offline. Job: see how much was collected, when and from where; pull any period as a report; find a business and print its demand notice.

World: brief-pinned to GovPayHub (Taraba State Central Billing System), the state's public portal. No direction roll: the user pinned the world; this is the operator-side counterpart in the same family. Build path: code-led (no image generation on this machine).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The calendar is the chart. The period you are looking at is chosen by touching the year's collection rhythm itself, not a dropdown; refuses the generic grid of KPI cards over a chart.

OWN-WORLD: GovPayHub grammar: forest band #0B3D2E with soft green radial glows, white card rising out of the band with a hairline outline shadow, warm off-white canvas, Nunito medium tracking-tight, uppercase muted sidebar section labels, hairline gap-px figure grid, primary green oklch(42% .068 160), section headers with a 2px primary rule over a hairline.

STORY: Manager lands, reads the collected total for the period as a sentence, sees Jan's peak and the taper, clicks a month, every figure and ranking rescopes. Officer goes to Reports, sets From/To dates, previews, downloads CSV or prints. Registry search opens a printable Consolidated Demand Notice.

White card overlaps band bottom by 64px: filter row, then hairline figure grid (collected, businesses paying, average, change vs previous period, assessed).
